# Works not included in the paper (page limit)

All **68** works that were collected and screened for the survey but could not be cited in the paper because of the page limit. Together with the 232 cited works they form the 300 references of [`data/references.csv`](../data/references.csv). Every title is clickable; the *Link* column says whether it opens the paper page, arXiv, a DOI or (where no stable link was recorded) a Google Scholar search.
